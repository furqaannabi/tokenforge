TERM LOAN AGREEMENT


THIS AGREEMENT is dated as of November 30, 2026, between ATLAS MACHINE TOOLS LIMITED (the "Borrower") and HARBOURSIDE LENDING PARTNERS (the "Lender").

1.1 Facility. The Lender advances the principal sum of $1,000,000.00 to the Borrower on the date of this Agreement.

1.2 Interest. Interest accrues at 10.00% of the principal amount over the term of the facility, computed on a 30/360 basis, and is payable in equal instalments alongside principal.

1.3 Amortisation. Unlike an interest-only facility, principal under this Agreement is repaid in five equal instalments of $200,000.00 rather than in a single sum at maturity.

1.4 Maturity. The final instalment falls due on May 31, 2029, by which date the facility shall have been repaid in full.

1.5 Payment Dates and Amounts. Each instalment comprises $200,000.00 of principal and $20,000.00 of interest, being $220,000.00 in total, payable on:

28 Feb 2027
31 Aug 2027
29 Feb 2028
31 Aug 2028
31 May 2029

Total repayable over the term: $1,100,000.00.

2.1 Late Payment. Sums not received within 15 days of the due date bear additional interest at 2.50% per annum.
