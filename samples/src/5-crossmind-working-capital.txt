WORKING CAPITAL FACILITY AGREEMENT


THIS FACILITY AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is dated as of October 15, 2026, between NORTHBRIDGE CREDIT PARTNERS, a Delaware limited partnership (the "Borrower"), and CROSSMIND, a company incorporated in India (the "Lender").

1. THE FACILITY

1.1 Principal Amount. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, Lender agrees to advance to Borrower the principal sum of $1,200,000.00 (the "Facility"), drawn in a single advance on the date hereof.

1.2 Interest. The principal amount outstanding from time to time shall bear interest at a rate of 9.00% per annum, computed on a 30/360 basis.

1.3 Maturity Date. The entire outstanding principal balance, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, shall be due and payable in full on October 15, 2028.

1.4 Payments. Borrower shall repay the Facility in eight equal quarterly instalments of principal of $150,000.00 each, together with interest accrued on the outstanding balance, commencing on January 15, 2027 and continuing on the fifteenth day of each April, July, October and January thereafter until the Maturity Date.

2. COVENANTS

2.1 Financial Reporting. Borrower shall deliver unaudited quarterly management accounts within 45 days of each quarter end, and audited annual financial statements within 120 days of each fiscal year end.

2.2 Minimum Liquidity. Borrower shall maintain unrestricted cash and cash equivalents of not less than $200,000.00 at all times.

2.3 Negative Pledge. Borrower shall not create or permit to exist any lien over its receivables without the prior written consent of Lender.

3. DEFAULT

3.1 Events of Default. Each of the following constitutes an Event of Default: (a) failure to pay any amount when due, which failure continues for seven (7) days; (b) breach of any covenant in Clause 2 which, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within thirty (30) days; (c) the commencement of insolvency proceedings in respect of Borrower.

3.2 Grace Period. No Event of Default shall arise under Clause 3.1(a) unless the relevant payment remains unpaid for seven (7) days after its due date.

3.3 Acceleration. Upon an Event of Default which is continuing, Lender may declare all amounts outstanding immediately due and payable.

4. GOVERNING LAW

4.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware.
